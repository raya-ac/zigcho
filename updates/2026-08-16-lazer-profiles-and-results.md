# lazer profiles and results

the game and website finally agree on what your account is doing. your in-game profile keeps the combined Stable + lazer stats, while kai.ovh lets you swap between combined, Stable, lazer and ScoreV2 without mixing up the actual play lists. lazer play time moves now too, including after a full stats rebuild.

leaderboards no longer act like every vanilla lazer play was NM. accepted vanilla mods share the normal board, Relax and Autopilot stay in their own spaces, and custom sets keep their exact board. the result screen now gives an overall placement on every status we rank, including qualified and loved maps, instead of working on some maps and silently dropping the rank on others.

modded star rating is calculated from the play for the website and score announcements instead of reusing the map's base stars. old supported Stable and lazer plays are repaired during the release, score details show the exact stars and mods, and play time shows from the first few seconds. official achievements use their real osu! medal art; our extra accuracy, pp and high-star achievements have their own labelled medals instead of borrowing the wrong official image.

Kai is properly online in lazer now and has the useful chat commands rather than one canned reply. duel invites, queue state and ranked rooms got another pass as well, and closing the lazer chat poll no longer turns into a fake online-server interruption.
