# lazer pp and shared boards

lazer pp finally follows the calculator built into our pinned lazer client instead of being dragged through the older Stable rules. the normal board also stops showing two copies of the same person now: Stable and lazer plays fight for one place, and whichever has the higher pp is the one you see.

Stable Relax and Autopilot plays show up properly in lazer too, including their actual mods and replay download when the server has the replay. i have not changed the Relax or Autopilot pp calculators here. this is the server fix only; nobody needs another client patch for it.
